# Importance guidance

Edit this file to tell the importance scorer what YOU consider notable in your radio traffic.
It is embedded verbatim into the scoring prompt — plain prose, no code. Restart not required;
it is re-read per scoring batch.

## What matters to me (raise the tier)

- **Severe weather**: tornado / funnel cloud / SKYWARN activation / flash flood / damaging wind /
  large hail reports — actual reports, not forecasts mentioned in passing.
- **Public-safety / emergency**: injury, vehicle accident, structure fire, medical emergency,
  search-and-rescue, mayday / pan-pan, "priority traffic", "break break" interrupting traffic.
- **Net activations for a real event**: ARES/RACES callout, emergency net standing up (NOT the
  routine nightly practice nets).
- **Unusual activity**: a normally quiet channel suddenly busy; coordinated activity across
  several channels at once.

## What is NOT important (keep routine)

- Routine nightly/weekly practice nets and their scripted preambles — including any that *mention*
  emergencies, 911, or "if this were an actual emergency" as part of the script.
- Ragchews, signal reports, radio-check / kerchunk tests, equipment talk, weather as small talk.
- Drills and tests explicitly announced as drills/tests.
- Repeater IDs and automated announcements.

## Tiers

- **0 routine** — ordinary traffic.
- **1 notable** — mildly interesting, worth a glance (unusual topic, first-time station on a quiet
  channel). Shown only in the player, never badged on the timeline.
- **2 important** — I would want to notice this: real weather reports, public-safety traffic,
  genuine net activations.
- **3 urgent** — active emergency / life-safety / mayday in progress.

When uncertain between two tiers, choose the LOWER one. False alarms erode trust faster than
missed low-tier items. This is a hobbyist monitoring aid, not a safety system.
